These are words you can do without reading or writing.
Shigesato Itoi

On Sunday evening, here I am, stuck with nothing but my own confusion. "Here" is my work desk, the place where I write my drafts. I begin typing without a single thought about what to write, simply because I don't want to be working on this late at night like usual. My goal is to be in bed by 10 PM on Sunday night. Why? Because I want to wake up around the time I normally go to bed—4 or 4:30 AM—and keep my eyes open for about three hours. I've got three things scheduled for Monday, so I prefer not to drag myself through the day sleep-deprived.

I’m trying something unusual: writing my draft for tomorrow during the day on Sunday, then having dinner, taking a bath, and going to bed early. As you're probably guessing, this is all so I can watch soccer. It's the World Cup match between Japan and the Netherlands. I'm watching with hope, but there's no guarantee we'll win. I'm familiar enough with the disappointment of losing. Even if I manage this early-to-bed, early-to-rise routine, the exhaustion from a loss would be overwhelming. As you might know, I’m not exactly in prime physical condition. Yet, I push myself to watch and cheer for the soccer broadcast. If that sounds self-important, please forgive me. What I'm doing is quite ordinary—just another day's story. Still, the weariness is real. I could just rest, record the match, and watch it later at my leisure. I know this. I've asked myself these questions. But I'm part of the "Yume ni Teashi wo" team. If I want to watch, what can I do now? If I don’t want it to interfere with work the next day, what should I do?

So, I write my draft on Sunday evening. Thankfully, I watched "The Toyotomi Brothers" past midnight on BS-4K. The daytime game between the Seibu Lions and Yomiuri Giants wrapped up quietly. (Even now, in this moment, the world and I keep moving…)
By the time you’re reading this, I wonder how it all turned out?

Thank you for visiting "Hobo Nikkan Itoi Shinbun" today. To dispel any doubt, I’m sending off this draft now.
