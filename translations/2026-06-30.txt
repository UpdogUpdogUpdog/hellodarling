Surely, "Oh, what a relief! What a relief!"
Shigesato Itoi

・I'm writing this a bit earlier than usual today.
  Naturally, it's because I'll be watching the soccer game that starts at 2 a.m.
  With the kickoff at 2 a.m., I'm always wide awake by then.
  In that sense, staying up late is like second nature to me.

  But that's not all there is to it.
  While staying awake is no problem, watching the game until 4 a.m. means I have no time to write "this!"
  I have to choose between writing in an excited state after 4 a.m. or getting "this!" done before the match starts.
  "This!" might look like a breezy piece of nonsense, but when it comes down to writing it, it's not that simple.
  Sometimes I try to dash it off in 20 minutes or attempt some speed writing, but I end up flustered and realize my lack of literary talent, finally giving up.
  I'd rather not struggle with anything other than soccer in the early morning, so I've decided to write "this!" in advance.

  Having made that decision, I felt like taking a bath early too.
  Lately, I've been trying to bathe and go to bed earlier.
  It's not as easy as it sounds, but I'm making an effort.
  So, I took my bath before 10 p.m. this time.
  Right now, my body feels nice and warm.
  Moreover, I'm all set to sleep whenever I feel like it; I've already brushed my teeth and changed into my pajamas.
  A glance at the clock shows it's now 11:17 p.m.
  And as you can see, I've managed to write "this!" up to about eighty percent.
  It's quite convenient how my ramblings and complaints end up completing the draft.
  I can't do this every day, but this time, it's really been a lifesaver.

  By the way, the update time for "this!" is 11 a.m.
  By then, the result of "that" will be out whether I like it or not.

Thank you for visiting "Hobo Nikkan" today.
Who knew a mere time difference of less than ten hours could hold such depth?
