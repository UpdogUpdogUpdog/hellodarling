The real show begins once your foolishness is exposed.  
Shigesato Itoi

・Throughout our lives, we encounter countless people.  
　We collaborate, observe one another, and share moments in the same time and place.  
　Our families, friends, colleagues, the shopkeepers we frequently visit, even the customers—all these people cross paths with us.  
　Some we met as children, others during our rebellious adolescence or early adulthood.  
　Some we fell in love with, and some before whom we made fools of ourselves.  

　No matter how much we try to puff ourselves up, dress ourselves with grandeur, or act wise and kind, those who have been with us for a long time generally see through it all.  
　Our shortcomings, faults, selfishness, slyness, and weaknesses—most of it is laid bare.  
　Those who have observed us closely over the years know what we're really like.  
　Even people who proclaim noble ideals often aren’t quite the same at home.  

　When you've met and spent time with so many people, when you've done and talked about so much, people tend to figure you out.  
　In essence, as you live longer, it becomes clear that your foolishness has long been exposed.  
　As you might have guessed, I’m talking about myself.  
　Especially as I sit here every day, pondering and writing even these short sentences, it's obvious that no matter what front I put up, I'm not much of anything special.  

　When did I start realizing this?  
　At some point, I gave up on certain things and started thinking this way: "Life truly begins once your foolishness is exposed."  
　Of course, I still have my unnecessary desires and ambitions, but I feel I've pretty much come to terms with them.  
　It seems that this acceptance allows for a bit more freedom… don’t you think?  

Thank you for visiting "Hobo Nikkan Itoi Shinbun" today.  
Well, for the most part, I've been in my everyday clothes all this time.
