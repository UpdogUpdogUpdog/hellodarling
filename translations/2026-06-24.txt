I want to bring the onsen version of myself back home.  
Shigesato Itoi

For years, I had a strict routine: take a bath right before bed. After I finished everything on my to-do list—work, reading, watching dramas—and there was nothing left, I'd go to sleep. That was my habit for decades. So, the bath naturally fell into the pre-sleep slot. It wasn't really something I looked forward to. Sure, soaking in hot water feels good, and there's nothing wrong with staying clean. But to me, it felt more like a chore. I never really lingered or relaxed much. I was usually tired, eager to finish and get to bed. It was the epitome of my rushed, no-time-to-spare lifestyle.

But when I visit an onsen, I'm a different person. I'm the king of leisure, the master of the open-air bath. My mood is relaxed, and I dip into the water over and over, soaking up the warmth and enjoyment. Work can wait—it's all about letting the hot spring wash away my fatigue and worries. I sigh as much as I want. I'm an onsen indulgent. It's such a stark contrast to my daily baths at home.

So, I decided I wanted to bring that onsen version of myself home. First, maybe the water at home was too lukewarm. A fresh start: crank up the temperature a bit. And with "Sleepy Bear's Forest Bath" salts, I added that onsen-like quality. Just those changes made a huge difference in how cozy it felt. I started to actually want to enjoy my bath. After much thinking, I realized that "before bed" wasn't the best time for a bath. It left me too rushed. (This phase of trial and error lasted several days…) By taking my bath around 9 or 10 PM, it started to feel a lot more like an onsen experience. And guess what? I could still do some work afterward! Plus, I even managed to start going to bed a little earlier.

Thank you for visiting "Hobonichi" today. Life habit experiments are tough to make stick, though, aren't they?
